Hi, I'm Liam! I'm a software engineer (4 YoE) and AI safety researcher
(0.25 YoE). SF-based since Jun 2026, NYC before that (2019–2026), Princeton
NJ before that (1997–2019). Currently coworking out of
[Mox](https://moxsf.com). I love to make complicated things as simple and
organized as possible and then improve them. In my career and hobbies I do
this with software systems and art projects, in personal conversations I do
this with emotions, organizations, and life decisions.

In the offseason I co-lead a burning man camp with my friends. We bring 30
people out into the desert every summer, keep them well-fed and well-shaded,
run a cafe in the mornings, and build a 24-foot tall geodesic sphere with
trampoline inside and a light-show on top. Check it out!
